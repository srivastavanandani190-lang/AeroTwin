## 2. Project Title

**AeroTwin: An Environmental Intelligence and Digital Twin Proof of Concept**

## 3. Problem Statement

Air pollution can contribute to respiratory and cardiovascular health concerns, but environmental dashboards alone do not represent an individual patient's changing health state. AeroTwin explores how environmental measurements and patient-related data could be combined to support proactive health monitoring.

## 4. Healthcare Use Case

AeroTwin analyses air-quality data and is designed to demonstrate environmental forecasting and a what-if personal exposure simulator. Its intended direction is to combine environmental exposure with historical patient records and time-series wearable measurements.

**Current limitation:** Environmental forecasting is not the same as predicting a patient's health event. Patient-specific health predictions require appropriate patient and wearable data, a defined outcome label, and independent model validation.
