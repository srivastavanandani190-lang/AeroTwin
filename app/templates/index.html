🏥 Clinical Grounding • CPCB Air Quality Index (AQI) Guidelines
Consensus Health Risk Stratification (0 to 500+ AQI)
Good (0–50) Minimal health impact for everyone.
Satisfactory (51–100) Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive patients.
Moderate (101–200) Discomfort to people with asthma & heart conditions.
Poor (201–300) Breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure.
Very Poor (301–400) Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
Severe (>400) Severe cardiorespiratory strain on all patients.
