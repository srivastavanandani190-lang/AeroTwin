CPCB Health Impact Guidelines

Good (0-50) Minimal health impact for everyone.
Satisfactory (51-100) Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
Moderate (101-200) Discomfort to people with asthma and heart disease.
Poor (201-300) Breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure.
Very Poor (301-400) Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
Severe (>400) Affects healthy people; severe impact on patients.

AeroTwin Studio: Environmental Overview, What-If Simulation & Forecasting

OBSERVED: GROUND TELEMETRY FORECAST: GRADIENT BOOSTED TREES SIMULATION: MASS-BALANCE
Methodological Decision & Scientific Integrity (Step 6 Audit & Step 7 Verification):
The Step 6 dataset audit confirmed that CPCB monitoring records track environmental ambient air quality only; no validated patient health-outcome labels or clinical endpoints exist. To uphold scientific integrity without training misleading diagnostic classifiers (Case B), AeroTwin provides: (1) an empirical 24-hour Next-Day PM2.5 forecasting model rigorously evaluated on unseen holdout data, and (2) a transparent What-If Micro-Environmental Personal Exposure Simulator using physical mass-balance equations.
