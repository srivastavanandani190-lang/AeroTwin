8. Data Quality, Governance & Clinical Transparency

SUMMIT INTEGRITY COMPLIANCE
Prototype Decision Support Model
Patient Cohort Data Fully synthetic cohort (5 archetypes). Generated deterministically based on clinical consensus distributions (ICMR/RSSDI South Asian T2D guidelines). No personal identifying health information (PHI) or real patient records were accessed.
Sensor Stream Data 5-minute sampled multimodal physiological telemetry (CGM glucose, HR, HRV SDNN, steps, sleep stage, sensor quality flags). Includes physiological meal absorption, dawn phenomenon, and GLUT4 exercise clearance dynamics.
Forecasting Target "Will interstitial glucose reach or exceed 180 mg/dL within the next 120 minutes?" Formulated as a clinical decision-support prototype. Evaluated strictly on forward held-out temporal data (Days 6-7).
Model Architecture Dual HistGradientBoosting framework: Binary excursion classifier (PR-AUC 0.998, Precision 98.6%, Recall 97.15%) + continuous 120-minute regressor (MAE 6.16 mg/dL vs Persistence Baseline 14.44 mg/dL).
CLINICAL INTEGRITY & SUMMIT TRANSPARENCY NOTICE:
This application is an engineering proof-of-concept for the Happiest Health Reimagining and Reforming Healthcare in India Summit 2026. The machine learning models and What-If physiological simulations are research decision-support prototypes and are NOT clinically validated medical devices. Outputs do NOT constitute a medical diagnosis, prescription, or clinical recommendation. Patients and clinicians must never adjust medication or insulin dosages based on simulated trajectories.
